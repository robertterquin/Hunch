# Visual Foundation

## Brand Direction

Hunch should feel like a clear, modern student safety tool. It should be practical, direct, trustworthy, and minimal.

The app should not look like a traditional marketing website. It should feel like opening a useful tool.

The visual direction is dark-first. Use deep neutral backgrounds, quiet borders, and restrained accent colors so the risk states stand out without making the whole app feel loud.

## Personality

- Calm
- Sharp
- Helpful
- Student-friendly
- Protective
- Interactive
- Minimal

## First Impression

When users open Hunch, they should immediately understand:

- This app checks OJT and internship posts.
- They can paste or upload something right away.
- The output will be a risk score with explanations.

## Visual Priorities

1. Analyzer first.
   The paste box and analyze action should dominate the first viewport.

2. Risk clarity.
   Risk score, risk label, and warning count should be easy to scan.

3. Explainability.
   Warning cards should feel readable and educational.

4. Action guidance.
   Checklist and next steps should be visually connected to the analysis.

5. Minimal distraction.
   Avoid decorative sections that do not help the student evaluate a post.

6. Dark interface clarity.
   The dark theme should improve focus, not hide information. Text contrast, field boundaries, and warning states must remain clear.

## Layout Mood

Use a clean dark app/dashboard layout:

- Focused top navigation
- Wide analyzer area
- Result panel beside or below the analyzer
- Compact status cards
- Clear section dividers
- Dense but breathable spacing
- Dark surfaces with subtle contrast between page, panels, and inputs

## Visual Motifs

Useful visual ideas:

- Signal strength
- Risk meter
- Checkpoints
- Verification markers
- Warning labels
- Evidence highlights

Avoid:

- Overly playful scam imagery
- Fear-based visuals
- Long hero illustrations
- Decorative gradients that reduce readability
- Marketing cards that push the analyzer below the fold
- Bright full-page warning colors
- Heavy shadows on dark surfaces

## Content Tone

Use short, direct text.

Good:

- "Paste an OJT post to check for red flags."
- "Training fee detected."
- "Verify the recruiter email before applying."

Avoid:

- "Harness AI to revolutionize your internship safety workflow."
- "This is definitely a scam."
- "Congratulations, this job is safe."
